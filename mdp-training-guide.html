Book the courtOutside the app

Before anything is posted, the court has to actually be reserved at the venue, and the ball boy arranged. The app doesn't do this — posting a day in the app does not book a court.

Leslie tells Meg which hours she's free → Meg confirms the court at Vantage → only then is the day posted.

3

Players bookIn the app

In the 🎓 Training tab, players pick an hour and book — alone, with another member, or with a guest.

If someone books an hour that already has one player in it, the booking waits for Leslie's approval so she can check the two are a good match. Nobody pays until she approves.

5

Confirming paymentsIn the app

Leslie confirms the coaching fees in her own app (Payments tab). Meg confirms the court and ball boy — for now that's just her own record-keeping, since those aren't in the app yet.

The app reads each receipt and gives a suggestion — green if everything matches, amber if something looks off. It's only advice. Always check your own GCash before confirming.

6

Cancellations

Coaching feeMore than 12 hours before = full credit. Less than 12 hours = forfeited.
Court feeMore than 48 hours before = full credit. Less than 48 hours = forfeited.
Leslie cancelsEveryone gets everything back as credit, whatever the timing.

12 hours before the session the list is final — no joining or dropping after that. It's the same moment cancellations close.

If your partner drops out more than 48 hours before, you're alone in that hour again and cover the court yourself (₱475 more). If they drop out closer than that, their forfeited payment covers it and you owe nothing.

7

Credits and refunds

  • Credits go to the player's wallet and never expire.
  • No automatic cash refunds. If someone wants cash, an admin pays them and records it with the 💸 Cash out button in Admin → Financials.
  • Coaching credits are used with Leslie. Club credits (court, ball boy) are used with the club. They stay separate.
  • Wallet balance can pay for court and ball boy. The coaching fee always goes straight to Leslie.
